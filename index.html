02 · The Problem

Heat is hitting a local wall.

Conventional cold plates are still mostly passive plumbing. The bottleneck is no longer total wattage — it is local hotspots, transient loads, dry-out, fouling, and flow imbalance. Failures cascade into throttling, shorter lifetime, oversized systems, and expensive downtime.

Future systems need cooling that behaves more like a living transport network.

05 · The Solution

A biomimetic flow architecture, packaged as a self-regulating module.

01 · Geometry
Biomimetic flow paths
Branching channels that distribute coolant the way leaves distribute water — minimizing dead zones and pressure spikes.
02 · Working fluid
Dielectric two-phase loop
Latent-heat cooling for high local flux without electrical risk near silicon and optics.
03 · Heat rejection
Compact condenser
High-density rejection in the same envelope, enabling drop-in replacement of legacy cold plates.
04 · Stability
Passive-first regulation
Geometry-driven flow balancing under transient loads — no controller in the critical path.
05 · Intelligence
Embedded sensing
Pressure, flow, and temperature signatures fused into early-warning diagnostics for dry-out and fouling.
06 · Manufacturing
Selective 3D printing
Internal geometries that conventional machining cannot reach, with controlled surface chemistry and leak-tight assembly.
2× hotspot capacity 10–20 °C cooler operation 25–40% lower thermal resistance no increase in pumping load
